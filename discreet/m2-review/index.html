02What Discreet does

An app that already talks to OpenAI uses Discreet by changing one setting. Checked with OpenAI's own Python library.

The appSends a normal chat request, plus a header saying what it's for (for example "claims summary").
DiscreetRefuses eligibility decisions · finds NRIC, phone, email, card, postal code, unit number, date of birth · swaps them for placeholders · keeps the real values encrypted for minutes · writes an audit record.
The AI modelA free mock for everyone; a real model only for holders of an access token, under a daily budget.
App sendsCall S1234567D on 9123 4567 about claim C-2041
AI seesCall <NRIC_1> on <PHONE_1> about claim C-2041
App gets back…S1234567D… 9123 4567… plus a footer saying personal data was replaced

The NRIC is the synthetic worked example from sg-pii-rules. Card numbers are redacted and never put back.

04The evidence

5,000 synthetic samples from a generator written separately from the rules, on purpose including ways of writing personal data the rules don't handle yet. A value counts as caught only if it's covered completely.

Recall per kind of personal data

Share of true values fully caught, on 5,000 samples (7,531 values). Every supported writing style scored 100%; the gaps are styles not supported yet.

Show as a table
EntityCaught / totalPrecision

What the misses are

  • NRICs written S 1234567 D or with hyphens
  • Phones written 91 23 45 67 or 912 34567
  • Cards with double spaces between groups
  • Emails written "name at example dot com"
  • Postal codes with no clue, or after "S'pore"
  • Unit numbers without #
  • Dates of birth after "Birthday", with words in between, or with a two-digit year

False alarms: 482, all bare eight-digit order and batch numbers read as phone numbers, the trade-off accepted in sg-pii-rules ADR 0007. No other kind had any. Precision overall 92.3%.
